What Constitutes a 3P Event?

Defining the scope, pillars, and strategy behind Amazon ZA's third-party seller events programme.

Definition

A 3P event is defined as a structured gathering of more than 10 Sellers that requires catering, a venue, and potentially travel if hosted outside of Cape Town.

Events are distinct from other Seller touchpoints such as one-on-one account manager calls, email campaigns, or self-serve training resources. The key differentiators are the physical or virtual gathering format, the minimum attendee threshold, and the logistical requirements they impose.

Events vs. Other Seller Touchpoints

Not all Seller interactions are events. The table below illustrates where 3P events sit relative to other touchpoints:

Touchpoint Format Min. Sellers Counts as Event?
Seller Roadshow In-person, large-scale 10+ ✓ Yes
Virtual Workshop Virtual, structured 10+ ✓ Yes
Sponsored 3rd-Party Event External venue, co-hosted 10+ ✓ Yes
AM Check-in Call 1:1 call 1 ✗ No
Email Campaign Async, digital Any ✗ No

Our Events Strategy

Our events strategy is organised into three pillars that together cover the full Seller lifecycle — from acquisition through to scale.

Pillar 01
Sponsored Events

Hosted by a third party that we leverage to gain access to a pool of potential Sellers. These events focus on new Seller acquisition and brand awareness for Amazon ZA.

🎓
Pillar 02
Virtual & In-Person Workshops

Targeted events with existing Sellers focused on FBA and Deals (Section 4). Designed to deepen Seller capability and drive adoption of key programmes.

🚌
Pillar 03
Seller Roadshows

Large-scale events designed as a touchpoint for SSR Sellers (Section 6). Focused on scale, retention, and establishing Amazon as the partner of choice.

KPIs & Success Measurement

Every event must have clear KPIs relevant to the event type. Events are less a support centre for managed Sellers and more a lever we will use to drive scalable 3P growth through SSR.

We will provide support to event owners in calculating these goals as well as ROI, and we are in the process of developing a ROI calculator to measure return on investment across event types.
