Amazon ZA 3P Events Playbook

Your single source of truth for planning, executing, and measuring third-party seller events in South Africa.

🚀 Explore Tools 📅 Upcoming Events

This wiki is your single source of information on all past and upcoming 3P events.

This page formally defines what constitutes a 3P event, provides an overall update on performance, shares seller anecdotes and key learnings, and outlines the strategy and planned events for the remainder of the year. It also provides clarity on goals and the org structure within the events team.

A structured, scalable approach to 3P events is critical to meeting Sellers where they are as our store matures, and to establishing Amazon as the partner of choice for South African Sellers.
